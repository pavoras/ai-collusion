\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\paragraph{What this means in practice.}
Each point is limited to this game.
(1)~\emph{Screening from public bids is feasible.} An overseer who sees
only bids, as competition authorities usually do, could have used a
calibrated alarm to point to the right pair in most markets where AI agents
coordinated actively, with few false alarms and no wrong accusations.
(2)~\emph{Calibrate before alarming.} The fixed threshold published with
the method flagged most or all honest markets; a permutation correction
fixed this. An uncalibrated screen would accuse innocent firms.
(3)~\emph{Use costs to understand, not to detect.} Costs did not improve
detection of talking cartels, but they reveal which way information flows
and separate a two-way cartel from a one-way spy when evidence is pooled.
That needs access to private data and many markets.
(4)~\emph{Always compare with a simple baseline.} A plain correlation did
as well for talking cartels; net influence earned its place only for
espionage. Reporting net influence alone would overstate its value.
(5)~\emph{Watch profits too, and do not trust a small AI judge.} The cartel
without chat earned the most and was nearly invisible to both measures. The
9B judge, given ample tokens, judged firm strength rather than coordination.

\paragraph{What the evidence does not show.}
Only the primary test is confirmatory; alarm counts and flow patterns are
pre-registered secondary results. The replication was planned after the
original results were known and reuses the honest control markets. Twenty
markets per type give wide intervals, and absence of an advantage is not
equivalence. A one-way leak is not a unique signature of espionage, and the
pooled flow intervals are approximate. The judge result concerns one 9B
model judging its own family. The probes cannot support a causal claim.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The auction is synthetic, the cartel is instructed rather than emergent,
and the treatment bundles a joint objective, partner information, a
surveillance warning and, in C1, a chat channel. The core uses one model;
small models were unusable and breadth cells have ten markets. Privacy,
false-accusation and dual-use considerations are discussed in
Appendix~\ref{app:ethics}.

\paragraph{Next steps.}
Independent replications; screens that combine correlation, flow direction
and profits; larger and more varied bidder and judge models; markets with
more firms; and probes with independent message draws per context.
