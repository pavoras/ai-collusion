\section{Conclusion}
Watching only the bids of AI agents, a calibrated net-influence alarm found
cartels that coordinated by chat and named the right pair, without wrong
accusations. With private costs, the measure also showed which way
information flowed, separating a two-way cartel from a one-way spy when
evidence was pooled. Yet a simple correlation detected talking cartels as
well, the most profitable cartel stayed hidden, and a small AI judge looked
at firm strength rather than behaviour. Behaviour-based screens can direct
an investigation; they do not establish agreement, intent or harm.
