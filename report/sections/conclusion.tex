\section{Conclusion}
Net influence detects the instructed strong-pair cartel treatment in the
registered experiment. Correlation is a demanding same-access baseline,
and the results show no general advantage over it. Espionage provides a
specific descriptive lead for further work. Reliable flow classification,
judge comparisons under broad coverage, and causal probe inference remain
unresolved. These methods can support investigation; they do not establish
agreement, intent, or harm.
